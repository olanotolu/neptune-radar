03 · the detective

10 providers. married-name variants. budget caps.

to find a mailing address for a couple we've never met, the system runs a cascade. it tries free sources first (bios, captions, business profiles), then fans out across paid and free people-search providers with a configurable budget cap. it tries married-name variants — person A with partner B's last name. it stops early when it finds a high-confidence street. every candidate is verified through the USPS.

05 · the output

a postcard. in their mailbox.

the end product is a 4×6 physical postcard. B&W editorial design. geist sans-serif headlines, iowan old style serif body, geist mono labels at 8px with 0.14em letter-spacing. printed and mailed via lob at ~90¢ per card. the front carries the couple's discovery photo. the back carries a handwritten-feel message. it arrives 3-5 days after the system first detected the marriage.
