# Neptune Radar

## What it is
An agentic CRM that watches authorized social and public-record signals for newly engaged and recently married couples, scores life events deterministically, and turns high-confidence signals into human-approved CRM actions — celebrate first (postcards), never auto-pitch.

## Who uses it
Internal operators at Neptune Growth OS. Power users who live in the tool all day: dense tables, keyboard shortcuts, fast queues. The product is a professional instrument, not a consumer app.

## Primary jobs
1. See what needs human attention today (approve, investigate, congratulate)
2. Pause / resume the radar and stay within spend budget
3. Build and mail physical congratulations kits with verified addresses
4. Maintain sources, map coverage, and data quality

## Product principles
- Celebrate first — soft congratulations before any hard invite
- Human in the loop for anything customer-facing
- Engagement signals ≠ relationship-risk signals (structurally separate)
- Model proposes; policy decides; operator approves

## Constraints
- React + Vite SPA, Go API, Postgres
- Auth via admin bearer token
- Functional status color only (green / amber / red on indicators)
- Brand visual world: pure black & white, Linear/Vercel August 2026 craft
