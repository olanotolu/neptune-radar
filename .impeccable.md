## Design Context

### Users
Internal operators at Neptune Growth OS — a wedding CRM used to discover
recently married couples via public records and social signals, verify them,
and send physical congratulations postcards. Operators are power users who
live in the tool all day: keyboard shortcuts, dense data tables, fast
navigation. The interface must feel like a professional instrument, not a
consumer app.

### Brand Personality
Disciplined. Precise. Quietly confident.

### Aesthetic Direction
Pure black & white — no decorative color. Black is the accent. Status colors
(green/amber/red) are functional only, used for dots and small indicators.
Geist Sans + Geist Mono typography at 15px base. Hairline borders, flat
surfaces, almost no shadows. The reference is Linear/Vercel August 2026 craft:
flat nav underline tabs, no backdrop blur, no translateY hover lifts, no
soft shadows, no emoji icons, no decorative gradients. Physical artifacts
(postcards) use a refined editorial serif (Iowan Old Style) for personal
warmth, paired with Geist Mono for labels. See DESIGN.md for the built system.

### Design Principles
1. Black is the only accent — never introduce decorative color
2. Hairlines over shadows — borders communicate structure, shadows are rare
3. Flat confidence — no hover lifts, no bounce easing, no decorative gradients
4. Functional color only — green/amber/red for status dots, nothing else
5. One radius scale — --radius-sm/--radius/--radius-lg/--radius-xl, no one-offs
6. Geist everywhere — sans for UI, mono for data/labels, serif only for physical artifacts
